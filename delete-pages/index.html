How to remove pages from a PDF

Sometimes a PDF has pages you do not want: a blank scan, a cover sheet, an old appendix or private information. This tool shows the pages, lets you click the ones to delete or type a range, and gives you a new PDF without them.

The original file is not changed, and nothing is uploaded, because the work is done in your browser.

How to use it

  1. Drop your PDF onto the upload area.
  2. Click the pages you want to remove, or enter a page range.
  3. Press Delete Selected Pages.
  4. Download the cleaned PDF.

Tips

  • Check the page thumbnails before deleting, so you remove the right pages.
  • If you want to keep only a few pages, the Split PDF tool is faster.
  • After deleting pages, use Add Page Numbers to renumber the document.
  • Keep your original file in case you need the removed pages later.

More questions and answers

Can I delete several pages at once?

Yes. Click multiple pages, or type a range.

Does the original file change?

No. You download a new file.

Can I undo a deletion?

Use Clear Selection before you press delete, or simply start again from the original file.

Is my file private?

Yes. It never leaves your browser.
