Tips

  • Extracting all pages gives one PDF per page, downloaded as a ZIP
  • Page range "2-5" extracts pages 2, 3, 4, 5 into a new PDF
  • Use commas for non-consecutive pages: "1,3,7" extracts three specific pages

How to Split a PDF for Free

Splitting a PDF is useful when you have a large document and only need certain pages — like extracting one chapter from an ebook, or pulling out a single page to send separately. PDFCraft splits PDFs entirely in your browser with no file size restrictions.
