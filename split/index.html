How to split a PDF or extract pages

Splitting a PDF means taking some of its pages out into a new file. Common reasons are sending one chapter of an ebook, sharing a single page of a contract, separating the pages of a scanned bundle, or reducing a file that is too large to upload.

This tool has two modes. Extract all pages turns every page into its own PDF and gives you the files together as a ZIP. Extract a page range builds one new PDF from the pages you choose. Everything happens in your browser, so nothing is uploaded.

How to use it

  1. Drop your PDF onto the upload area.
  2. Choose a split mode: all pages or a page range.
  3. For a range, type pages such as 2-5 for a continuous block or 1,3,7 for specific pages.
  4. Press Split PDF and download the result.

Tips

  • A range of 2-5 gives you pages 2, 3, 4 and 5 in a new PDF.
  • Use commas to pick pages that are not next to each other.
  • To remove pages instead, use the Delete Pages tool.
  • To put the pieces back together later, use Merge PDF.

Frequently asked questions

How do I extract one page from a PDF?

Choose Extract a page range and type the page number, for example 4.

Can I split a PDF into separate pages?

Yes. Choose Extract all pages and download the ZIP.

Does splitting reduce quality?

No. Pages are copied as they are.

Is there a limit on file size?

There is no server limit because nothing is uploaded. Very large files depend on your device memory.
