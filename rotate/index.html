Why Rotate a PDF?

PDFs often end up with incorrect page orientation when scanned on a flatbed scanner, exported from mobile apps, or received from others. PDFLab lets you rotate PDF pages in seconds — fix a single page, a range, or all pages at once.

How to rotate pages in a PDF

Pages end up sideways or upside down when a document is scanned on a flatbed scanner, photographed with a phone or exported from an app. This tool rotates the whole document or only the pages you choose, by 90, 180 or 270 degrees, and saves a corrected PDF.

Rotation is saved in the file, so the pages open the right way up in every PDF reader, on phones and on printers.

How to use it

  1. Drop your PDF onto the upload area.
  2. Choose the angle: 90 degrees clockwise, 180 degrees, or 270 degrees (90 counter-clockwise).
  3. Choose All pages or Specific pages and enter the page numbers.
  4. Press Rotate PDF and download the corrected file.

Tips

  • If only one scan is sideways, choose Specific pages and enter that page number.
  • To turn a page the other way, use 270 degrees instead of three clockwise rotations.
  • Rotating does not change how sharp the page is.
  • After fixing orientation, use Merge PDF or Add Page Numbers to finish the document.

Frequently asked questions

Can I rotate just one page of a PDF?

Yes. Choose Specific pages and enter the page number.

Which angle turns a page counter-clockwise?

Use 270 degrees.

Will rotating reduce quality?

No. Only the page orientation changes.

Is my file uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser.
