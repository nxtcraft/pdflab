How to sign a PDF without printing it

Signing a PDF no longer needs a printer and a scanner. Draw your signature on the screen with a mouse, trackpad or finger, upload the PDF, choose the page and the position, and download a signed copy. You can pick the ink colour, and set the width of the signature to match the signature line.

The signature is added as a picture on the page. It works for forms, rental agreements, consent letters and similar documents where a simple signature is accepted. For legal papers that require a certified digital signature or Aadhaar eSign, use those services instead.

How to use it

  1. Draw your signature in the box, and choose dark blue, black or dark red ink.
  2. Upload the PDF you want to sign.
  3. Choose the page (the last page, or a page number) and a position such as bottom right.
  4. Adjust the signature width and press Sign and Download.

Tips

  • Draw slowly in a large box for a cleaner signature.
  • Place the signature just above the line rather than over text.
  • Dark blue ink looks closer to a real pen signature on most documents.
  • Check the signed file before you send it.

More questions and answers

Is a drawn signature legally valid?

It depends on the document and the party who receives it. Many agreements accept a simple signature image, but some need a certified digital signature or Aadhaar eSign.

Can I sign on my phone?

Yes. Draw with your finger and place the signature in the same way.

Is my signature stored anywhere?

No. It is used only in your browser and is not uploaded.

Can I sign on a specific page?

Yes. Choose the page number or the last page.
