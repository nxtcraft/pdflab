How to convert images to a PDF

Many forms and portals accept only PDF files, while documents often arrive as photos or screenshots. This tool converts JPG, PNG or WEBP images into a single PDF. Select several images at once to combine them, and choose the page size: A4, Letter or fit to each image.

Images are scaled to fill the page while keeping their shape. Common uses are scanned documents, ID card photos, WhatsApp screenshots and receipts. The conversion is done in your browser, so your images are not uploaded.

How to use it

  1. Select one or more images.
  2. Choose the page size, usually A4.
  3. Press Convert to PDF.
  4. Download the PDF.

Tips

  • Select all images at once so they are combined into one PDF.
