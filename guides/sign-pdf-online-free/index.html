How to Sign a PDF Online for Free

You can sign a PDF on your computer or phone without printing it. This guide shows the steps and explains when a simple signature is enough and when you need a certified digital signature.

Open the PDF signer →

Step by step

  1. Draw your signature in the box and choose an ink colour.
  2. Upload your PDF.
  3. Choose the page and the position, for example bottom right on the last page.
  4. Set the width and press Sign and Download.
  5. Open the signed PDF and check the placement.

Tips for a good signature

Draw slowly in a large box. Dark blue ink looks natural on most documents. Keep the signature above the signature line and away from printed text.

When a drawn signature is enough

Many private forms, consent letters and agreements accept a simple signature image if both sides agree. Some documents need a certified digital signature (DSC) or Aadhaar eSign instead. Check what the receiver asks for.

Privacy

The signature and the PDF stay in your browser and are not uploaded.

Frequently asked questions

Is a drawn signature on a PDF legally valid?

It depends on the document. Some need a certified digital signature or Aadhaar eSign.

Can I sign a PDF on my phone?

Yes. Draw with your finger.

Is my signature stored?

No.
