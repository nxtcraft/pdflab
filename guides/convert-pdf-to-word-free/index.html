How to Convert a PDF to Word for Free

Converting a PDF to Word lets you edit the text, but the result depends on the type of PDF. This guide explains what works, what does not and how to get a usable file.

Open the PDF to Word converter →

Step by step

  1. Open the PDF to Word converter.
  2. Drop a text-based PDF onto it.
  3. Check the preview.
  4. Download the .docx file and open it in Word or Google Docs.

Which PDFs convert well

Documents that are mostly text, such as reports, articles and contracts, convert well. Brochures, multi-column layouts and pages full of pictures often lose their design because a PDF stores the position of each item rather than paragraphs.

Scanned PDFs

A scanned PDF is a photograph of a page. It has no text to extract, so you need OCR software to read it. This converter does not perform OCR.

Tips for a better result

Reformat headings and tables in Word after converting. If you only need a few lines, use the PDF text extractor and paste the text. If you only need to fix a word or two, edit the PDF directly.

Frequently asked questions

Can I convert a scanned PDF to Word?

Not with this tool. Scans need OCR.

Why does my Word file look different?

PDFs do not store paragraphs and tables the way Word does.

Is the PDF uploaded?

No. The conversion runs in your browser.
