How to Compress a PDF to 100 KB, 200 KB or 1 MB

Government and exam portals, job sites and email systems often limit upload size. If your PDF is too big, this guide shows how to reduce it to the size you need and what to check before you upload.

Open the PDF compressor →

Choose the right approach

If you know the limit, use Target Size, enter the number, choose KB or MB and press Compress. If you only want a smaller file, pick a quality level: Low gives the smallest file, Medium is balanced and High keeps the best quality.

Step by step

  1. Open the Compress PDF tool and drop your file onto it.
  2. Choose Target Size and type the maximum, for example 200 and KB.
  3. Press Compress PDF and wait for your browser to finish.
  4. Check the new file size and open the file to confirm that it is readable.
  5. Upload the compressed file to the portal.

What affects the result

Scanned documents and PDFs with photos shrink the most, often by half or more. PDFs that are mostly text are already small. Long scans with many pages may not reach 100 KB without becoming hard to read, so split them or lower the quality only for the pages that need it.

Things to know

The tool re-renders pages as images to reduce size, so the text in the compressed file cannot be selected. Keep the original for copying text. Do not compress the same file again and again, because every pass lowers quality.

If it is still too large

Use Low quality, split the document into two parts with the Split PDF tool, or remove pages that you do not need with Delete Pages.

Frequently asked questions

How can I reduce a PDF to 100 KB?

Use a target size of 100 KB, or Low quality. Very long scanned documents may need to be split.

Will compressing make my PDF blurry?

Lower quality settings reduce sharpness. Check the result before uploading.

Is the compressor free and private?

Yes. It runs in your browser, and the file is not uploaded.
