How to rearrange the pages of a PDF

Scans often come out in the wrong order, or a document needs a different structure after you merge several files. This tool shows every page as a thumbnail so you can drag pages into the right order, or move a selected page one step at a time with the up and down buttons.

When you save, you get a new PDF with the pages in your chosen order. The work happens in your browser and the file is not uploaded.

How to use it

  1. Drop your PDF onto the upload area.
  2. Drag pages to new positions, or use the arrow buttons to move one step.
  3. Check the order, or press Reset to start again.
  4. Press Save New Order and download.

Tips

  • Move the pages that are furthest out of place first.
  • Use Rotate PDF to fix sideways pages before you reorder.
  • Use Delete Pages to remove duplicates.
  • Use Add Page Numbers after reordering so the numbers match the new order.

More questions and answers

Can I move a page to the first position?

Yes. Drag it to the start, or use the arrows.

Can I undo my changes?

Press Reset to return to the original order.

Does reordering change page content?

No. Only the order changes.

Is the file uploaded?

No.
