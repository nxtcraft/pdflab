How to Compress a PDF to 200 KB

  1. Open Compress PDF. The Target Size tab opens with 200 KB filled in.
  2. Choose your PDF or drop it on the page. Nothing is uploaded; the work happens in your browser.
  3. Press Compress. The tool tries quality levels until the file fits under 200 KB, keeping the sharpest one that fits.
  4. Check the final size shown, then download. Open the file once to make sure every page is readable before you upload it.

A 200 KB limit suits one or two pages, such as a marksheet, a caste or income certificate, or an address proof.

How Many Pages Fit in 200 KB?

QualityDense A4 pages that fit in 200 KB
Sharp (easy to read small print)about 1 page
Readable (normal text is clear)about 2 pages
Smallest the tool can makeabout 3 pages

Measured on a full page of dense text. Pages with more white space, such as most forms and certificates, take less room, so more of them fit. Colour photos and dark scans take more.

If the PDF Won't Go Under 200 KB

  • Remove pages you don't need. Blank pages, instructions and duplicate copies all add size. Delete pages →
  • Split it. Many portals accept several smaller files. Split PDF →
  • Rescan in black and white at 150 DPI instead of colour at 300 DPI. A colour phone photo of a page can be 2–5 MB; a black-and-white scan of the same page is a fraction of that.
  • Making a PDF from photos? Compress the images first with ImgLab, then combine them with Image to PDF.

What Changes When You Compress

To reach a fixed size, PDFLab turns each page into a compressed image. The pages look the same, but the text inside can no longer be selected or searched. That is fine for uploading proofs and certificates. If you need searchable text, keep the original as well. Password-protected PDFs must be unlocked first with Unlock PDF.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I compress a PDF to 200 KB for free?

Open PDFLab Compress PDF, choose Target Size, enter 200 KB, add your PDF and press Compress. It is free, needs no signup, and the file never leaves your device.

How many pages can a 200 KB PDF have?

About 2 dense A4 pages at readable quality, or up to 3 at the lowest quality. Pages with more white space fit more.

Will compressing to 200 KB reduce quality?

Only as much as needed. The tool picks the highest quality that still fits under 200 KB. Small print can blur at very low sizes, so open the file and check it before uploading.

Is it safe to compress Aadhaar, PAN or bank statements here?

Yes. PDFLab runs entirely in your browser; your PDF is not uploaded to any server.
