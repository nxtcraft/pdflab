How to Remove a PDF Password

If you have a password-protected PDF that you need to access without entering the password every time, PDFLab's PDF password remover lets you unlock it instantly — no software, no upload, no risk. Your file is decrypted entirely inside your browser.

Common uses: unlocking PDF bank statements you save monthly, removing passwords from payslips or tax documents, unlocking government-issued PDFs (Form 16, ITR acknowledgements, Aadhaar cards that are password-protected with your date of birth).

Aadhaar & government PDF passwords

Many government PDFs use a standard password format. For example, Aadhaar card PDFs from UIDAI use the first 4 letters of your name (uppercase) + your birth year, e.g. PRIY1995. Enter that in the password field above to unlock it.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I remove a password from a PDF?

Upload your PDF, type the current password, click Unlock PDF, and download the unlocked file. That's it.

Can this tool crack a PDF password I don't know?

No. You must know the existing password. This tool removes a known password so you don't have to type it every time — it cannot bypass or brute-force unknown passwords.

Is my PDF safe?

Completely. Everything happens in your browser's memory. No file and no password is ever sent to any server.

What's the Aadhaar PDF password?

UIDAI Aadhaar PDFs are locked with: first 4 letters of your name in capitals + birth year. Example: if your name is Priya and you were born in 1990, the password is PRIY1990.
