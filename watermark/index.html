How to add a watermark to a PDF

A watermark marks a document as a draft, a copy or confidential, and can carry a company name. This tool stamps your text across the pages of a PDF. You control the text, the font size, the opacity, the rotation and the colour, and you can apply it to all pages, odd pages, even pages or only the first page.

Typical uses are DRAFT on a proposal, CONFIDENTIAL on an internal report, SAMPLE on a template, or a business name on a quote. The file is processed in your browser, so confidential documents are not uploaded.

How to use it

  1. Drop your PDF onto the upload area.
  2. Type the watermark text, for example CONFIDENTIAL.
  3. Set the size, opacity, rotation and colour.
  4. Choose which pages to stamp and press Add Watermark, then download.

Tips

  • An opacity of 15 to 25% keeps the page readable.
  • Short all-capital words such as DRAFT work best.
  • A rotation of -45 degrees gives the usual diagonal look.
  • For a company name on A4, a font size of 60 to 80 px works well.

More questions and answers

Can I watermark only some pages?

Yes. Choose All pages, Odd pages, Even pages or First page only.

Can I add an image watermark?

This tool adds text watermarks.

Will the watermark be removable?

It is added to the page content, so treat it as a visual marker rather than a security feature.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. It is processed on your device.
